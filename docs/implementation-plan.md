## Execution and evidence rules

All 82 changes form **one stacked dependency chain**. Each change starts from
the finished tree of the immediately preceding change. There is no independent
branch join. A later change may incrementally update an ancestor's modules;
ownership does not mean duplicating or bypassing their contracts. The first
change starts from the inspected baseline.

The chain orders source work, not phase acceptance. A later source change may
proceed while an earlier phase's physical or media evidence is unavailable;
the earlier gate remains open until all its criteria pass. No source test,
fixture, demonstration, or placeholder substitutes for a phase exit. A
regression reopens the affected gate; changes to source, content, dependencies,
or environment invalidate affected evidence.

Each change includes focused success, failure, and boundary tests for its
behavior. Test-only encounter fixtures remain outside the player distribution.
The production executable exposes the canonical fresh-campaign route, not
fixture selectors, credit-editing flags, or unlocked shortcuts. Native
Windows adapters need real Windows cgo checks; portable tests do not establish
native compatibility.

Dependency locks, vendored files, expanded manifests, hashes, and build evidence
are generated by checked commands, not invented by writers. Intake must verify
the proposed Go 1.27.3, raylib-go v0.55.1, GCC 14.2.0, and MinGW-w64 12.0.0
pins, their archives, compatibility, security, and licenses. An unavailable or
unsuitable input requires an explicit reviewed replacement and renewed checks
before baseline freeze.

No project command or qualification scenario has been evidenced as run for
this baseline. Required final exports, rights records, and physical or
specialist qualification are unconfirmed. Missing prerequisites are **Blocked**;
scenarios not yet attempted are **Not run**. Neither status is Pass.

## Campaign and intermediate slice

The canonical route begins with hospital theft, heart attack, funeral
resurrection, and the subsidy bargain. It then visits hospital, church,
ministry, and institute, returns to the ministry for the permit and consent,
continues through Hell, and ends at the hospital ceremony.

Each institution has four families—SUP, RES, UTL, and PRV—with six distinct
authored tickets per family. Thus 4 × 4 × 6 gives **96 service tickets**.
The closures `D-HOS-END`, `D-CHU-END`, `D-MIN-END`, and `D-INS-END` add four
distinct credits. The same campaign reaches 25, 50, and 75 at the first three
closures, 99 after the institute service batches, and 100 only when
`D-INS-END` certifies the restored public-address line after the institute boss
is defeated. Boss victories, repeated tickets, enemies, refills, and ending
scenes award no additional credit.

The permit requires all 100 receipts and NAR-09, not debt repayment or an
extra deed. Consent is cancellable before its durable commit. Authorized
death, Satan's rejection, the health-minister appointment, resource diversion,
casino ribbon, visible burning children's hospital, Nina's evacuation, and
the exact closing text form the one canonical ending. The mandatory tableau
must be acknowledged even when scenes are skipped; replay is read-only.

PH-02 is an **intermediate integration check**, never the deliverable. Its
production route includes the playable prologue and six hospital SUP tickets.
It also needs functioning representative RES, UTL, and PRV examples; limb,
feces, combat, Potroshin attack/protection/recovery/retry behavior; an
interrupted sixth-receipt emergency; and input-to-presentation, commit, death,
and Continue evidence. Isolated examples may use test-only fixtures. PH-02
acceptance also requires representative **final-quality** character, bay, UI,
music, and effects, and acceptance of the production pipeline. Development
fixtures or temporary media cannot close it. PH-03 completes the boss and
hospital rather than treating the representative boss as complete.
